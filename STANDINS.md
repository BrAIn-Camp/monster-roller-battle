# Stand-in reference

Generated by `tools/make-standins.mjs` from `critters/critters_*.js` and `critters/slots.json`. Do not edit by hand; re-run the script after any change to the critters.

Each of the 251 slots has an original critter that shares three things with its counterpart: the species category, the types and the Pokédex height (taken from the open PokéAPI data set; Fairy is dropped throughout because it did not exist yet, and Steel is dropped from the first 151 for the same reason, while slots 152-251 keep Dark and Steel, which arrived with them). Names and designs are original and deliberately different. Slots 1-151 live on Crossway Continent and were built by hand; slots 152-251 live on Lanternrise and are put together from a dozen body plans by `tools/make-critters-152-251.mjs`.

Size is the critter's longest side. The ball can roll a critter up once the ball is 2.5 times that wide.

## Size note

Only four of the first 151 are over 2.5 m (3.5 m, 4.0 m, 6.5 m and 8.8 m), and three of the next hundred (3.8 m, 5.2 m and 9.2 m), so a ball of about 6 m can roll up almost every critter.
Growth beyond that comes from scenery: boats, sheds, trees and houses.
