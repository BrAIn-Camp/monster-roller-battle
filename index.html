How to play

  1. Roll over anything up to half your ball's size to pick it up and grow. A green ring on the ground means you can roll it up now; a broken red ring means not yet.
  2. A critter with a pulsing yellow ring is only a little too big: dash into it to stun it, then roll it up before the stars round its head run out.
  3. Ram a smaller ball to knock its critters loose. Follow the beam of light to events.
  4. After five minutes your best six fight in an arena. They are along the bottom of the screen. Three of a kind merge into the next stage, so collect in threes: the ring round a portrait fills as you get close.
  5. Everything else you roll up makes those six stronger. The hexagon beside them grows a corner for each stat, and the number in its middle is how much stronger they are. Collect fast critters for Speed, tough ones for HP and Defence.
  6. The arena has no walls. Your ball knocks a monster further the more hurt it is, so finish the weak ones off the edge (dash into them for a smash). Roll through your own monsters to set off their signature moves. Keep your ball on the floor: if it falls, it is gone.
