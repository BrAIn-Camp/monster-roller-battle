How to play

  1. Roll over anything up to half your ball's size to pick it up and grow.
  2. Ram a smaller ball to knock its critters loose. Follow the beam of light to events.
  3. After five minutes your critters fight in an arena. Three of a kind merge into the next stage first, so collect in threes.
  4. The arena has no walls. Ram rivals off the edge, and roll through your own monsters to set off their signature moves. Keep your ball on the floor: if it falls, it is gone.
