Simple steering — WS roll, AD turn (or arrows); gamepad left stick
Tank steering — WS left hand, IK right hand; gamepad both sticks
Sprint — hold Shift (gamepad A or RT): more than twice as fast and very hard to steer. Crash at speed and you drop things, more the faster you were going
Mouse — moves the camera: left and right swing it round the ball, up and down tilt it, wheel zooms. Click to capture the pointer so it never runs out of screen.
M mute · add ?mute to the address to start silent
T switch steering (now: simple) · Esc / Start to pause
Gamepad on this screen: A = play a round, X = free roam
